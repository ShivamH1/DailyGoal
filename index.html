Match rules — why this holds up

One subject per morning

Maths Tue, ML Wed, DL Thu, Stats Fri. Depth beats juggling — and the order follows the dependency chain: maths feeds ML, ML feeds DL.

Intensity waves, not flat effort

Hard Mon & Thu (home workouts), easy Tue & Fri (walks), moderate Wed (run). Daily movement works only because the effort rises and falls.

Friday tapers into the weekend

Walk and stretch on Friday, matches Sat–Sun. Eight hours of cricket is the real training load — the week is built around it.

Mornings are for the hard subjects

One fresh hour at 6:45 beats three exhausted hours at 10 PM. AI & ML is cumulative — the maths must be understood, not memorised.

Don't break the chain — but never twice

Missing one day is a rain delay, not a lost season. The rule: never miss two days in a row. That single rule protects every streak.

Sleep is the umpire

7.5 hours, non-negotiable. If soreness lingers past two days or match performance dips, swap a hard day for a walk — don't drop the habit.
